# tampermonkey-scripts

## Scripts

### [indeed.js](./indeed/indeed.js)

Auto-dislikes job postings from specific companies on page load. This seems to be necessary in the post-AI job market since Indeed advertises thousands of "jobs" that are actually spam to the typical job-seeker not looking for data-labeling/annotating part-time 1099 "jobs", and Indeed's "dislike" button doesn't permanently hide the disliked job posting, plus the dislike doesn't get applied to identical job listings by the company.
